# 🍍 My Favorite Series: SpongeBob SquarePants

> "I'm ready! I'm ready! I'm ready!" — SpongeBob SquarePants

## 📖 Overview
**SpongeBob SquarePants** is an iconic American animated comedy television series created by marine science educator and animator Stephen Hillenburg for Nickelodeon. Set in the underwater city of Bikini Bottom, it follows the daily misadventures of an enthusiastic, optimistic yellow sea sponge and his quirky aquatic friends.

## 🌟 Main Characters
* **SpongeBob SquarePants** - An energetic, joyful sea sponge who works as a fry cook at the Krusty Krab.
* **Patrick Star** - SpongeBob's loyal, lovable, and dim-witted best friend.
* **Squidward Tentacles** - A grumpy octopus who lives next door and despises his neighbors' noise.
* **Mr. Krabs** - The money-obsessed owner of the Krusty Krab and SpongeBob's boss.
